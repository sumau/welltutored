# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Parents and students, equally.** A parent choosing a tutor for their
  daughter and a female secondary or A-level student looking for help both
  read the public site, often together. Copy and design must work for either
  reader without talking down to the student or past the parent.
- **Tutors** maintain their own profile and the Resources they write, and read
  their Enquiries, in the private Workspace.
- **The Owner** manages every Tutor profile and approves Workspace Accounts.

## Product Purpose

Well Tutored helps families find women tutors and send an Enquiry to a named
Tutor. Success is a visitor finding a Tutor who fits and sending an Enquiry.
The Enquiry is the main thing a visitor does.

## Positioning

- Every Tutor is a woman, and the students are young women. The Tutors are
  role models as well as teachers.
- A small, hand-picked group of named Tutors, not a searchable marketplace.
- Each Tutor has deep expertise in her subject, shown through her university
  background and qualification.
- The chosen Tutor reads each Enquiry and replies herself. There is no instant
  booking.

## Operating Context

- Lessons are online, as visitors expect, so students can be anywhere, in
  the UK or abroad; the copy needn't say either. The Public Origin is
  welltutored.co.uk.
- Visitors flow: Home → Tutor profile → Enquiry. No account is needed.
- Tutors and the Owner sign in to the Workspace (Clerk) to edit profiles,
  Resources and Enquiries.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Terminology follows the repo's `GLOSSARY.md`: Tutor, Directory, Resource,
  Enquiry, Intake (`accepting` / `limited` / `full`), Availability, Workspace,
  Owner. Avoid the words it lists under each term.
- A `full` Tutor cannot receive Enquiries.
- Resources are hidden from visitors while a feature switch is off.
- Tutor profile edits are held as a draft until published, so editing never
  changes what visitors see.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real Tutor profiles: name, subject, university, qualification, summary,
  Experience, Education, Availability, Intake.
- Logo and favicon: `frontend/public/logo.svg`, `frontend/public/favicon.svg`.
- None of these exist, so they must not be invented: testimonials, reviews,
  student results, exam-grade statistics, numbers of students, press. Rates
  on profiles are labelled illustrative, so don't present a confirmed price.

## Product Principles

1. The Tutors are the product. Lead with the named women and what they know,
   not with generic tutoring promises.
2. Personal, not a platform. A small group and a human reply, never the scale
   or automation of a marketplace.
3. Speak to the parent and the student at once.
4. Claim only what is true. With no testimonials or results to show, trust
   comes from the Tutors' real backgrounds and plain, honest copy.
